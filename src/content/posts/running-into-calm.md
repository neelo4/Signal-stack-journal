### Pre-run: wake the joints and prime the breath

Give yourself eight minutes before you even lace up. Start with cat-cow stretches, then glide into ankle circles and hip openers. Follow with three rounds of box breathing—inhale for 4, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold empty for 4. This stabilizes the nervous system so your run begins smooth instead of frantic.

### During the run: cadence over speed

Choose a playlist that keeps you under 150 BPM. Match your cadence to the beat and aim for nose inhales, mouth exhales. Check in every kilometre: are your shoulders creeping up, is your jaw tight? Shake them out. Treat each stride as a brushstroke painting blood flow back toward your skin. The goal is consistency, not burning out.

### Mid-run reset: sensory checkpoints

Halfway through, slow to a jog and take a panoramic look at your surroundings. Pick one colour, one scent, and one sound. Naming them out loud interrupts spiralling thoughts and embeds the run as a mindful experience.

### Post-run: cool, cleanse, and replenish

Walk for five minutes to bring down your heart rate, then stretch calves, hamstrings, and hips while breathing deliberately. Rinse with lukewarm water first, then finish with a cool splash across your face to constrict vessels and enhance glow. Follow with a magnesium-rich snack and a hydration boost (electrolytes + cucumber slices). Your skin and nervous system will thank you at tomorrow’s mirror check-in.
