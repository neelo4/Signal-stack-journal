### 90 minutes before lights out: downshift the senses

Dim the overhead lights or switch on a salt lamp so your brain registers the sunset cue. Replace doomscrolling with chamomile or tulsi tea and let the warmth slow your inhales. Your melatonin production loves this margin.

### Cleanse + cushion the barrier

Cleanse with a creamy balm to dissolve SPF, then mist a mineral-rich toner. Layer a peptide or ceramide night mask while your skin is slightly damp. The occlusive finish locks in hydration and gives your barrier all night to repair micro-tears from the day.

### Soothe the mind-body loop

Slip into bed with a ten-minute body scan meditation. Start at your toes and breathe softness into every muscle. Keep a journal on the nightstand; write the thoughts that try to follow you into sleep. Once they’re on paper, your brain stops cycling through them.

### Final cue

Add a whisper of lavender or neroli oil to your pulse points, then turn off the light. You’ve told both your skin and your nervous system that rest is happening now—and they respond with deeper sleep and a smoother morning complexion.
