### Sip on mineral-laced water

Pour a glass of room-temperature water, add a pinch of mineral salt or electrolyte powder, and drink slowly. The trace minerals replenish what caffeine and meetings pull out, keeping cognitive fog and skin dullness at bay.

### Breath wave to reset cortisol

Set a three-minute timer. Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 2, exhale for 6. Repeat. This longer exhale signals your vagus nerve to downshift the stress response. Shoulders drop, jaw softens, and your body re-enters rest-and-digest mode.

### Sensory cue for steady focus

Finish with a tactile or aromatic reset—mist a calming facial spray, roll a citrus or vetiver oil between your palms, or simply step outside and feel sunlight on your eyelids. One intentional sensory moment tells your mind it’s safe to pause, so you return to work brighter, not burnt out.
