### Choose an oil that mirrors your scalp’s needs

Begin by warming your base oil between your palms. I love a blend of cold-pressed sesame for circulation, coconut for softness, and a few drops of rosemary or brahmi to stimulate the follicles. If your scalp tends to inflame, add blue tansy or tea tree to calm it down. The scent alone signals your body that a ritual is starting.

### Map pressure points with intentional strokes

Part your hair into four sections. Using the pads of your fingers—not your nails—press the oil into the scalp with small circular motions. Start at the temples to release jaw tension, glide along the crown to brighten brain fog, then work down to the nape where cortisol hides during long workdays. Spend a full minute on each zone so the oil absorbs and your nervous system registers the touch as safety.

### Lengthen and braid for overnight nourishment

Once the scalp is saturated, smooth leftover oil through the mid-lengths and ends. Gently detangle with a wide-tooth comb, then braid loosely. This keeps the hair from tangling while you sleep and helps the oil penetrate without sitting heavily on the roots. I wrap a silk scarf over my pillowcase—it keeps heat in, prevents friction, and turns the night into a mini spa session.

### Seal the ritual with a mindful rinse

In the morning, emulsify a sulfate-free shampoo with water in your hands before massaging at the scalp. Rinse with lukewarm water, then condition from mid-length to ends. Finish with a cool rinse to close the cuticle and lock in shine. As you towel dry, press the fabric gently instead of rough rubbing. Notice how your mind and scalp feel calmer? That’s the power of oiling—you’ve nourished your hair, but you’ve also taught your body to exhale.
